## UX ruling: sign-in, the 20 login parity cases

**Verdict: the shipped sign-in is the reference.** Nothing shipped wrong. The Paper login frames are stale for these 20 cases and may be re-anchored to the app at `dd57ce20531ff7f2ffa471548810d1eba312da3e`.

Cases: `login.invalid`, `login.loading`, `login.offline`, `login.unavailable` and `login.github-disabled`, each in light and dark, desktop and phone.

The shipped screen is my design on this issue ([comment 7006714995](https://github.com/MarkTripoli/bakery/issues/241#issuecomment-7006714995)), built in #713, which I approved at `06d489381` ([sign-off](https://github.com/MarkTripoli/bakery/pull/713#issuecomment-7021870751)). The Paper frames were drawn before that design. I opened all 20 app captures beside all 20 references. These are the differences, and each one is sanctioned:

| # | Cases | Difference from the Paper frame | Why the app is right |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | all 20 | A "Forgot password?" link at the end of the Password label row. | Section A of the design; acceptance criterion 1 of this issue. Without it a person who forgot their password has no next step. |
| 2 | all 20 | Under the button: "No account? Bakery accounts start from an invitation." with the link "How to get one" on its own line. Paper has "New to Bakery? Join through an invitation" (or "Back to sign in" on its offline and unavailable screens). | Section A. It says up front that there is no sign-up form. |
| 3 | all 20 | The theme button is in the top end corner of the form panel (desktop) or of the page (phone). Paper has it floating under the form. | Section A; the floating button was one of the faults this issue was opened for. |
| 4 | desktop, all 10 | The form block sits 25 to 28px lower. | A result of 3: the block is still centred, and it no longer contains the theme button. |
| 5 | `login.invalid` | The red alert is under the fields, directly above Sign in. Paper has it above the fields. It reads "That email and password do not match. Try again." Paper: "Email or password is incorrect. Please try again." | Section A: errors show one at a time in the form's alert under the fields, next to the button the person presses next. The copy is the design's. |
| 6 | `login.offline` | The sign-in form stays, with a warning under the fields: "You are offline. Reconnect, then sign in." Paper replaces the form with a "You're offline" screen (Try again, Back to sign in). | Section A: the message sits in the form, where the person is, and what they typed is kept. The separate screens were removed by the design. |
| 7 | `login.unavailable` | The same as 6, with "Bakery could not be reached. Try again." Paper has a "Sign-in is unavailable" screen. | Same reason as 6. |

Everything else matches the frames: the brand pane and its logo in both themes, the title and subtitle, both fields and their placeholders, the show-password button, the Sign in and "Working…" buttons, and the background and divider colours (same values in the capture and the frame, light and dark). No capture has clipping, overflow or a scrolling phone page.

**Limits.** This covers only these 20 cases and only the differences listed. A case that differs for any other reason is not re-anchored under this ruling; report it here and I rule on it separately. If the app at the pinned commit differs from dd57ce20 in any file under `apps/web/src` or `packages/ui/src` that sign-in uses, send me the new captures first.
