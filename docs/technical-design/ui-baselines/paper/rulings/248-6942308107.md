## UX design ruling: class A of the #248 recapture (settings pages)

1. **The shipped settings layout is the reference.** The grouped two-column
   settings navigation with the workspace sidebar is my #232 design, merged in
   PR #341 (b6818d165). The Paper frames that show a breadcrumb with horizontal
   tabs are stale for these 13 screens: backup-codes, clients, passkey-add,
   passkeys, password, revoke, revoke-client, security, sessions,
   totp-challenge, totp-disable, totp-setup, two-factor.

2. **Repinning those baselines to the current app is allowed, as a metadata-only
   change,** for every case whose difference from its Paper frame is the #341
   layout: the navigation, the breadcrumb, the removed tabs, and the content's
   position and width that follow from them.

3. **This ruling does not cover a case whose content differs for another
   reason** (text, a control, a state, an error, clipping). Such a case is not
   repinned under this ruling; it is reported on this issue and I rule on it
   separately.

Two conditions before the repin PR merges:

- dev-bugfix checks all 178 cases, not a sample, and lists any that do not fit
  point 2. A repin would hide them.
- I look once at stills from the app commit being pinned: one light desktop
  still for each of the 13 screens, plus dark and phone for security and
  passkeys. I reply on the repin PR.
